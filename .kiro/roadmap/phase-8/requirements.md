# Phase 8 Requirements

Status: readiness, mock, coverage-audit, and correction foundations locally complete; production course and approvals pending
Last reviewed: 2026-10-17

## R1: Complete reviewed course

Every approved skill must have source-traced instruction, retrieval practice, independent application evidence, and remediation across multiple activity types. Production release requires technical, instructional, accessibility, lab, product, and operations approval. Draft fixtures and unreviewed mappings cannot satisfy this requirement.

## R2: Transparent readiness

Readiness must be deterministically derived from recent versioned evidence using versioned rules. Skill summaries must expose evidence IDs, types, freshness, sufficiency, and missing evidence. Guided work, completion, confidence, multiple-choice, or mock evidence alone cannot establish the highest level. Readiness is not an exam-pass prediction or certification guarantee.

## R3: Timed mock experience

Mock sessions use original content, explicit timing, pause/restart recovery with an unchanged deadline, no hints, append-only evidence, missed-item review, and weak-skill drill paths. A preview cannot be represented as a complete exam simulation.

## R4: Governance and correction

Course releases record source basis, skills, review decisions, limitations, next review, correction owner, and rollback status. Released manifests require all approvals. Learners can submit authenticated, idempotent, privacy-minimized content reports through an explicit retryable workflow.
